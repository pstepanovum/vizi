# Vizi Privacy Policy

_Last updated: August 2026_

Vizi is an AI vision companion for blind and low-vision users. Privacy is a
core product principle: Vizi should feel like a trusted companion, and that
requires collecting as little as possible.

## What Vizi processes

- **Camera frames.** While a session is active, individual camera frames are
  sent to Google's Gemini API to understand your surroundings and answer your
  questions. Frames are processed transiently and are **never stored** by
  Vizi — not on your device beyond the moment of use, and not on our servers
  (we run none).
- **Voice.** Your speech is transcribed on your device by iOS speech
  recognition. Only the resulting text of your question is sent to Gemini.
  Vizi's spoken answers are generated by Fish Audio from the answer text.
- **Conversation context.** A short rolling window of the current
  conversation (text only) is kept in memory during a session to make
  follow-up questions work. It is discarded when the session ends.

## Your account

Signing in with Apple or Google creates a Firebase Authentication account
used only to identify you across devices and to attach your Vizi Plus
subscription (managed by RevenueCat and Apple). We store no profile data
beyond what the sign-in provider shares (name, email).

## What Vizi does not do

- No permanent storage of images or audio.
- No advertising, no tracking SDKs, no sale of data.
- No processing while the app is in the background.

## Subscriptions

Vizi Plus purchases are processed by Apple and managed through your Apple ID.
Subscription status is handled by RevenueCat. You can manage or cancel at any
time in your Apple ID subscription settings.

## Third-party services

Vizi relies on: Google Gemini API (vision and answers), Fish Audio (voice
synthesis), Firebase Authentication (sign-in), RevenueCat (subscriptions),
and Apple's on-device speech recognition. Each processes only the data
described above, under their respective privacy policies.
